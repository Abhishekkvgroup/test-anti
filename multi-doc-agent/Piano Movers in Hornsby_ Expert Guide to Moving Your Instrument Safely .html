Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much do piano movers in Hornsby charge?

Local moves cost $250 to $400 for upright pianos. Long-distance moves range from $400 to $700. Prices depend on stairs, distance, and how hard it is to access your piano.

2. Do I need to empty my piano before moving?

Yes. Remove all sheet music, books, and loose items from inside and on top. Lock the keyboard lid to protect the keys during transport. This prevents damage and makes the piano lighter and safer to move.

3. Can piano movers handle stairs in Hornsby homes?

Yes. Professional upright piano movers are trained to navigate stairs safely. They use special equipment like ramps and straps.

4. How long does it take to move a piano in Hornsby?

Most local moves take 1.5 to 2 hours. This includes wrapping, loading, transport, and unloading. Long-distance moves can take 3 to 5 hours or more. The time depends on distance and access to both homes.

5. Is my piano insured during the move?

Reputable piano movers in Hornsby carry goods-in-transit insurance. This covers accidental damage to your piano and your property. Always ask for proof of insurance before signing any contract. Good companies show this information upfront.

6. When should I tune my piano after moving?

Wait 2 to 3 weeks after the move. This lets your piano adjust to the new temperature and humidity. Then schedule a professional tuner. Even careful moves can affect the strings and sound quality slightly.

7. Can I piano transport myself to save money?

It's not recommended. Pianos weigh 135 kg or more and have delicate internal parts. Moving one yourself risks serious injury and costly damage. Professional piano movers in Hornsby have proper training, equipment, and insurance. The small cost is worth protecting your instrument and your health.
